# Licensing FAQ

## Is myTbot open source?

Yes. myTbot is licensed under AGPL v3.

## Can individuals use it?

Yes. Individuals may use myTbot under AGPL v3 and subject to the safety disclaimer.

## Can companies evaluate it?

Yes. Companies and institutions may evaluate myTbot under AGPL v3.

## Can companies use it commercially?

AGPL v3 permits commercial use if the user complies with AGPL obligations. Organisations that need proprietary, closed-source, hosted or enterprise terms outside AGPL v3 should contact Emfasys Ltd for a commercial licence.

## Can companies keep modifications private?

It depends on how the software is used and distributed. AGPL v3 has source-sharing obligations, especially for modified network services. Organisations requiring private proprietary modifications should seek a commercial licence.

## Does Emfasys accept external code contributions?

Not currently. Issues are welcome, but Pull Requests are not accepted.

## Who publishes myTbot?

myTbot is published by Emfasys Labs, a division of Emfasys Ltd.

Emfasys Labs may publish open-source tools, open research and source-available systems.

myTbot itself is an open-source project under AGPL v3.
